Steady

Your phone is a ladder-safety partner

Steady uses your phone’s motion sensors to get the ladder angle right, test for wobble, and raise the alarm if you fall.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no tracking, works offline.

No motion sensors found, so you’re in demo mode. Use the sliders and buttons to see how Steady responds. Open this page on a phone to use it for real.
